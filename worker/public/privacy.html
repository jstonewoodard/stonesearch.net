2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Network information — your IP address (which we may truncate or hash before storage), approximate location, and ISP.
  • Device and browser information — browser type and version, OS, screen size, language, and a hashed identifier from your user-agent.
  • Usage information — pages visited, results clicked, dwell time, referring URL, and date/time of interactions.
  • Cookies, local storage, and similar identifiers — for session, preferences, abuse prevention, and performance measurement.
  • Performance and crash data — anonymous error logs and latency metrics.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Third-party search providers — Brave, Bing, Google Programmable Search, etc. They receive your query and may receive your IP.
  • Advertising networks — click data when you interact with sponsored results.
  • Public sources — cached previews of public web pages.

  • Support advertising — contextual ads, and (unless you opt out) interest-based ads via partners.

6. Advertising and Sponsored Content

The Service is supported in part by advertising. Ads may be contextual (based on the query alone) or interest-based (based on signals from advertising partners over time).

You can limit interest-based advertising through:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance
  • Browser-level Global Privacy Control (GPC)
  • Device-level controls (Limit Ad Tracking on iOS, Reset Advertising ID on Android)

We honor Global Privacy Control as a request to opt out of “sale” and “sharing” of personal information under U.S. state privacy laws.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

  • Strictly necessary — operate the Service, maintain session, prevent abuse, load-balance.
  • Functional — remember preferences (theme, language, safe-search level).
  • Analytics and advertising — measure usage and (where permitted) deliver interest-based advertising.

You can refuse non-essential cookies in our cookie banner and via browser controls.

  • Abuse and security records — as long as reasonably necessary.
